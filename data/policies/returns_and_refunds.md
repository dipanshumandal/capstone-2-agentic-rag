# ShopEase Returns and Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

## Return window

Most products can be returned within 10 days of delivery. Electronics and mobile phones have a 7 day return window. Grocery, personal care items, innerwear and customised products cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged or were the wrong item.

The item must be unused, in its original packaging and with all tags and accessories. A return request is raised from My Orders > Return Item. A pickup is scheduled within 2 business days and the customer does not pay anything for the pickup.

## Refund methods

Refunds go back to the original payment method by default:

- UPI, debit card, credit card and net banking: 5 to 7 business days after the returned item passes the quality check
- ShopEase Wallet: instant credit once the pickup is done
- Cash on delivery orders: refunded to the bank account entered by the customer, 5 to 7 business days

The customer can choose a wallet refund instead of a bank refund at the time of raising the return. Wallet refunds are the fastest option.

## Tracking a refund

The refund status is visible in My Orders > Refund Status. The stages are: Return requested, Picked up, Quality check, Refund initiated, Refund completed. Once the status is "Refund initiated", the bank can take up to 7 business days to show the money. If it has not arrived after 7 business days the customer should share the refund reference number with support so we can follow up with the bank.

## Damaged, defective or wrong items

If the product arrived damaged, defective or is not what was ordered, the customer should raise the return within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the product and the packaging. In these cases a replacement is offered first. If a replacement is not available a full refund including the delivery charge is given.

## Non returnable situations

A refund is not given when the item has been used, when the tags have been removed, when the return is raised after the return window, or when the item was bought in a "final sale" with a no return label on the product page.
