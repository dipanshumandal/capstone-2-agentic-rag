# ShopEase Customer Support, Complaints and Escalation

Last updated: September 2026

## How to contact us

- Chat: the support chat in the app and website, available 24 hours
- Phone: 1800-123-4567 (toll free), 9 AM to 9 PM, Monday to Saturday
- Email: support@shopease.com, replies within 24 hours
- ShopEase Plus members get a priority line: 1800-123-9999

For order related issues the quickest way is My Orders > Need Help, because the order details are attached automatically.

## Talking to a human agent

If the chat assistant is not able to solve the problem, the customer can type "talk to an agent" and the chat is transferred to a human support executive. Transfer wait time is usually under 5 minutes during support hours. Outside support hours a ticket is created and an agent calls back the next working day.

## Complaints

Complaints about a product, a delivery partner or a support interaction can be raised from My Account > Help Center > Raise a Complaint. Every complaint gets a ticket number in the format CS-XXXXXX. A first response is given within 24 hours and most complaints are resolved within 3 business days.

## Escalation levels

1. Level 1: support executive (chat, phone or email)
2. Level 2: support team lead, if the ticket is not resolved in 3 business days
3. Level 3: grievance officer, email grievance@shopease.com, reply within 7 business days

The grievance officer details are also published on the website as required under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.

## Reviews and feedback

Product reviews can be written from My Orders > Write a Review after the item is delivered. Reviews are published after a short moderation check. Feedback about the service can be given from the thumbs up / thumbs down buttons in the chat, or by replying to the feedback email sent after every support interaction.
