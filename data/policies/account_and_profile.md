# ShopEase Account, Profile and Privacy

Last updated: September 2026

## Creating an account

An account can be created with a mobile number or email address from the Sign Up page. An OTP is sent for verification. One mobile number can be linked to only one account. There is no charge for creating an account.

## Account types

- Standard account: free, available to everyone
- ShopEase Plus: Rs. 499 per year, gives free express delivery on all orders, early access to sales and priority customer support

An account can be upgraded to Plus from My Account > Membership. The Plus fee is non refundable after 7 days of purchase. Downgrading to the Standard account is possible at any time and the Plus benefits stay active until the end of the paid period.

## Editing the profile

Name, email, mobile number, password and addresses can be changed from My Account > Profile. Changing the mobile number or email needs an OTP on the new contact. A password must have at least 8 characters with one number and one special character.

## Forgot password

Click "Forgot password" on the login page, enter the registered email or mobile number, and a reset link / OTP is sent. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes. If the registered mobile number is no longer accessible, the customer has to contact support with a government ID to verify the account.

## Switching between accounts

The app supports only one logged in account at a time. To use another account the customer has to log out and log in again. Orders cannot be transferred between accounts.

## Deleting an account

An account can be deleted from My Account > Delete Account. Pending orders, open returns and wallet balance must be settled first. Deletion is permanent and takes 30 days to complete, during which the customer can log in again to cancel the deletion. After deletion the personal data is removed, but invoices are kept for the period required by law.

## Registration problems

If the OTP does not arrive, wait 60 seconds and use "Resend OTP". Check that the number does not have DND (do not disturb) enabled for transactional messages. If the email is already registered, use "Forgot password" instead of signing up again.

## Newsletter

The customer can subscribe or unsubscribe from the promotional newsletter in My Account > Communication Preferences, or by clicking "Unsubscribe" at the bottom of any newsletter email. Order related emails cannot be turned off.
