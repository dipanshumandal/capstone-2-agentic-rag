# ShopEase Shipping and Delivery Policy

Last updated: September 2026

## Delivery options

ShopEase offers three delivery options at checkout:

| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard delivery | 4 to 7 business days | Free on orders above Rs. 499, otherwise Rs. 49 |
| Express delivery | 2 to 3 business days | Rs. 99 |
| Same day delivery | Same day if ordered before 12 PM | Rs. 149, only in Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Chennai and Pune |

Business days are Monday to Saturday. Public holidays are not counted.

## Delivery period

The delivery period starts from the day the order is dispatched, not from the day the order is placed. Most orders are dispatched within 24 hours. Orders placed on Sunday are dispatched on Monday.

If an order has not arrived within the promised window plus 2 extra days, the customer can raise a "delayed delivery" request from My Orders and the delivery charge is refunded automatically.

## Tracking an order

Every dispatched order gets a tracking number by SMS and email. Customers can track the order from My Orders > Track, or by entering the tracking number on the courier partner's website. Tracking updates can take up to 12 hours to appear after dispatch.

## Shipping address

The shipping address can be changed from My Orders > Change Address only while the order status is "Processing". Once the order is "Dispatched" the address cannot be changed and the customer has to either accept the delivery or refuse it so it is returned to us.

A new shipping address can be saved from My Account > Addresses. Up to 5 addresses can be saved per account. The pin code is validated at the time of saving, and some remote pin codes are not serviceable for same day or express delivery.

## International shipping

ShopEase currently delivers only within India. International shipping is not available.

## Failed delivery

The courier makes 3 delivery attempts. If all 3 fail the order is returned to the warehouse and the order amount is refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days, minus the delivery charge if any.
