# ShopEase Payment Methods and Payment Issues

Last updated: September 2026

## Accepted payment methods

- UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM and any UPI app)
- Debit cards and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express)
- Net banking from all major Indian banks
- ShopEase Wallet
- EMI on credit cards for orders above Rs. 3000 (3, 6, 9 and 12 month plans)
- Cash on delivery (COD) for orders up to Rs. 5000, with a Rs. 30 COD handling fee

International cards are not accepted at the moment. Gift cards can be redeemed into the ShopEase Wallet and then used for payment.

## Payment failed but money was deducted

Sometimes the bank deducts the amount but the payment confirmation does not reach us, so the order shows as "Payment pending". In this case the amount is automatically reversed by the bank within 3 to 5 business days. The customer does not need to raise a request for this. If the money is not back after 5 business days, the customer should share the transaction reference (UTR) number with support.

## Payment declined

A card or UPI payment can be declined when the bank blocks online transactions, the daily limit is crossed, the OTP was entered late, or the card has not been enabled for online use. We recommend trying a different payment method or contacting the bank.

## Double charge

If the customer was charged twice for one order, the duplicate amount is reversed automatically within 7 business days. The customer can also raise a "Double payment" ticket from My Orders > Need Help with both transaction reference numbers and the reversal is pushed on priority.

## Security

ShopEase does not store full card numbers. Card details are handled by our payment partner Razorpay and are PCI DSS compliant. Customers should never share OTPs or card PINs with anyone, including people claiming to be from ShopEase support. ShopEase support will never ask for an OTP.
